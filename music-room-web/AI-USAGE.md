# AI assistance

AI tools were used as development assistance for parts of the Sonora project. They
helped with code suggestions and adaptations, debugging, and work on CI/CD workflows
and tests. They supported the developer; they did not replace the developer’s
decisions or responsibility for the project.

Suggestions were reviewed and adapted as part of development. Builds, automated
tests, CI results, and manual checks provide separate evidence and should not be
treated as interchangeable validation.
